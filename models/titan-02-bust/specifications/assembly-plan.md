# V0.1 assembly plan

Use X for left/right, Y for front (negative) and rear (positive), and Z for up. The frame sits at Z = 0 mm.

1. Lower H002 along −Z over H001. Align the unequal tab widths with their corresponding roof pockets. The crown top seats against the inner roof. The nominal seated position leaves 0.5 mm at the pocket ends.
2. Slide H003 along +Y into H001's front sockets. Seat the mask back against the frame face. The pockets leave 0.5 mm at the tab ends.
3. Withdraw H003 toward −Y and lift H002 toward +Z.

The joints are straight slip fits. Retention under handling, seating repeatability, and preferred clearances depend on the physical print. Record printer, material, orientation, layer height, measured tab and socket sizes, insertion feel, and any damage under test_prints/.
